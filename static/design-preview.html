Design Preview — Analog Direction

A home for my thoughts & ideas.

This page is a working preview of three design ideas: a paper & film-grain substrate, an analog crosshair cursor, and hand-drawn marginalia. Move your mouse around the page. Hover over a link like this and notice how the cursor swells. The grain is continuously re-seeded so it feels like real film.

What to feel

The whole page should feel like a page from a field notebook rather than a screen. The serif (Cormorant Garamond) stays; the paper tone, grain, and hand marks are what shift the voice. Effects respect prefers-reduced-motion, and the cursor falls back to the OS pointer on touch devices.

Try the toggles at the bottom to compare each layer on and off.
